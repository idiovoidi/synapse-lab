NEUROTRANSMITTER SIMULATION - FUNCTIONAL OVERVIEW
===============================================

1. NEUROTRANSMITTER TYPES AND PRIMARY FUNCTIONS
---------------------------------------------
DOPAMINE
- Function: Reward and pleasure signaling
- Effects: 
  * Increases happiness level
  * Moderately increases arousal
  * Associated with reward-seeking behavior
- Interactions:
  * Enhanced by norepinephrine
  * Regulated by GABA
  * MAO sensitive (degraded by MAO)

SEROTONIN
- Function: Mood and emotional regulation
- Effects:
  * Increases happiness level
  * Decreases anxiety
  * Stabilizes mood
- Interactions:
  * Balanced by GABA
  * MAO sensitive
  * Can be depleted by excessive release

GLUTAMATE
- Function: Primary excitatory neurotransmitter
- Effects:
  * Increases neural activity
  * Enhances signal strength
  * Critical for learning/memory
- Interactions:
  * Counterbalanced by GABA
  * Not MAO sensitive
  * Rapid reuptake system

GABA
- Function: Primary inhibitory neurotransmitter
- Effects:
  * Decreases anxiety
  * Reduces arousal
  * Calming effect
- Interactions:
  * Counteracts glutamate
  * Not MAO sensitive
  * Modulates other neurotransmitter effects

NOREPINEPHRINE
- Function: Arousal and attention
- Effects:
  * Increases arousal significantly
  * Moderate anxiety increase
  * Enhances alertness
- Interactions:
  * Enhances dopamine effects
  * MAO sensitive
  * Can be inhibited by GABA

2. REUPTAKE AND DEGRADATION MECHANISMS
------------------------------------
MAO (Monoamine Oxidase) Degradation:
- Affects: Dopamine, Serotonin, Norepinephrine
- Degradation Rates:
  * Dopamine: 60%
  * Serotonin: 70%
  * Norepinephrine: 65%

Reuptake Systems:
- Specific transporters for each type
- Reuptake Rates:
  * Dopamine: 80%
  * Serotonin: 70%
  * Glutamate: 90%
  * GABA: 60%
  * Norepinephrine: 75%

5. INTERACTION RULES
------------------
Synergistic Effects:
- Dopamine + Norepinephrine = Enhanced reward response

- Glutamate + Norepinephrine = Enhanced learning potential

Antagonistic Effects:
- GABA vs Glutamate
- Serotonin vs Norepinephrine (anxiety effects)
- GABA vs Norepinephrine
