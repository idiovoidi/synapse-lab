# Visual Guide - What You'll See

## 🎨 Overall Appearance

### Background & Atmosphere
- **Deep space blue** background (almost black)
- **Subtle fog** in the distance
- **Cinematic lighting** from multiple angles
- **Soft shadows** under all objects

---

## 🧠 Main Structures

### Neurons (Orange/Peach Colored)
**Left Side - Presynaptic Neuron:**
- Large cell body (soma) with glowing blue nucleus inside
- Branching dendrites extending outward
- Long axon with white myelin sheaths (insulation)
- Bulbous axon terminal at the synapse

**Right Side - Postsynaptic Neuron:**
- Similar structure mirroring the left
- Dendrite terminal facing the synapse
- Receptors embedded in the membrane

**Look for:**
- Warm, organic glow from neurons
- Blue-glowing nuclei visible through transparent soma
- White myelin segments along axon
- Flowing blue particles inside axon terminal

---

## 🎯 The Synaptic Cleft (Center)

### Main Cleft Structure
- **Blue cylindrical space** between neurons
- **Wireframe overlay** for depth
- **Glows brighter** when more neurotransmitters are active
- About 0.8 units wide (the gap)

### What's Inside:
1. **Pink adhesion molecules** - Connect the two neurons
   - Thin strands with spheres at ends
   - 12 molecules around the periphery

2. **Tiny blue ECM particles** - Extracellular matrix
   - 50 small floating spheres
   - Represent proteins and molecules in the space

---

## 💊 Neurotransmitters (The Stars!)

### Appearance
- **Colorful glowing spheres** (0.25-0.3 units diameter)
- **Metallic sheen** with slight transparency
- **Pulsing glow** that breathes in and out
- **Cast shadows** on nearby objects

### Colors by Type:
- 🟣 **Purple** = Dopamine
- 🟠 **Orange** = Serotonin  
- 🔵 **Blue** = Glutamate
- 🟢 **Green** = GABA
- 🔴 **Red** = Norepinephrine

### What to Watch:
1. **Release** - Pop into existence with scale-up animation
2. **Drift** - Float across cleft with wavering motion
3. **Bind** - Snap to receptor and grow 20% larger
4. **Glow** - Pulse gets brighter when bound
5. **Unbind** - Shrink back and drift away
6. **Reuptake** - Get pulled into orange transporters
7. **Degrade** - Break apart near red enzymes

---

## 🎯 Receptors (Right Side of Cleft)

### Structure (Y-Shaped Proteins)
- **Gray cylinder** - Transmembrane channel (through membrane)
- **Gray sphere** - Binding site (where NT attaches)
- **10 receptors** arranged vertically
- **Metallic appearance** with subtle glow

### States:
1. **Inactive** (Gray)
   - Waiting for neurotransmitter
   - Subtle gray glow

2. **Bound - Excitatory** (Red)
   - Bright red color
   - Intense red glow
   - Both cylinder and sphere light up

3. **Bound - Inhibitory** (Green)
   - Bright green color
   - Intense green glow
   - Both parts change color

**Look for:** The dramatic color change when binding occurs!

---

## 🔄 Reuptake Transporters (Orange Channels)

### Structure
- **Orange cylinder** - Main channel body
- **Orange donut ring** - Opening at entrance
- **Metallic/glossy** appearance
- **8 transporters** around the cleft

### States:
1. **Active** (Orange)
   - Normal orange color
   - Moderate glow
   - Pulling neurotransmitters back

2. **Inhibited** (Red)
   - Bright red color
   - Intense glow
   - Not functioning
   - Simulates drug effects (like SSRIs)

**Look for:** Neurotransmitters getting sucked into the channels!

---

## 🔴 MAO Enzymes (Red Floating Structures)

### Structure
- **Large red sphere** - Enzyme core
- **3 small orange spheres** - Active sites (catalytic regions)
- **15 enzymes** floating around the cleft
- **Wobbling motion** - Shows they're active

### Function
- Break down certain neurotransmitters
- Only affects MAO-sensitive types:
  - Dopamine ✓
  - Serotonin ✓
  - Norepinephrine ✓
  - Glutamate ✗
  - GABA ✗

**Look for:** 
- Neurotransmitters breaking apart near enzymes
- Small red particles flying off (degradation products)
- Wobbling motion of enzymes

---

## 🌀 Special Effects

### Axon Terminal Pulse
- **When:** Each time you click "Release"
- **What:** Left terminal briefly expands and contracts
- **Duration:** 300ms
- **Purpose:** Shows the release event

### Synaptic Cleft Glow
- **Intensity:** Based on number of neurotransmitters
- **Color:** Blue (0x4466ff)
- **Effect:** More activity = brighter glow
- **Purpose:** Shows overall synaptic activity level

### Axonal Flow Particles
- **Location:** Inside left axon terminal
- **Appearance:** 20 small blue glowing spheres
- **Motion:** Circular flowing pattern
- **Purpose:** Shows active transport in neuron

### Neurotransmitter Pulse
- **Frequency:** Continuous
- **Pattern:** Sine wave (smooth breathing)
- **Speed:** 5ms period
- **Effect:** Glow intensity varies 0.1-0.3

## 💡 Lighting Effects

### Key Light (Top-Right)
- Brightest light
- Creates main shadows
- Warm white color

### Fill Light (Left)
- Softer, cooler blue
- Fills in shadows
- Prevents pure black areas

### Rim Light (Behind)
- Warm orange tone
- Highlights edges
- Creates depth separation

### Spotlight (Above)
- Focused on cleft
- Blue tint
- Dramatic emphasis

**Result:** Professional cinematic look with excellent depth

## 🎭 Visual Hierarchy (What Draws Your Eye)

### 1. Brightest - Active Elements
- Pulsing neurotransmitters
- Bound receptors (red/green)
- MAO enzyme active sites

### 2. Medium - Functional Structures
- Transporters
- Unbound receptors
- Enzymes
- Cleft boundaries

### 3. Subtle - Background
- Neurons (soma, axon, dendrites)
- Myelin sheaths
- Adhesion molecules

## 🎨 Color Palette

### Warm Tones (Organic/Living)
- Neurons: Peach/orange (0xffbb77)
- Nuclei: Blue-purple (0x5555cc)
- Myelin: White with blue tint

### Cool Tones (Extracellular/Space)
- Cleft: Blue (0x88aaff)
- Flow particles: Light blue (0x88aaff)
- Background: Deep blue-black (0x0a0a0f)

### Accent Colors (Activity)
- Transporters: Orange (0xff9933)
- Enzymes: Red (0xff6666)
- Active sites: Orange-red (0xffaa88)

### Signal Colors (Binding)
- Excitatory: Red (0xff6666)
- Inhibitory: Green (0x66ff66)
