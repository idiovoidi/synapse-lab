# Product Overview

An interactive 3D educational simulation that visualizes neurotransmission at a synapse. Users can release different neurotransmitter types (Dopamine, Serotonin, Glutamate, GABA, Norepinephrine) and observe their behavior in the synaptic cleft, including diffusion, receptor binding, reuptake, and degradation.

## Key Features

- Real-time 3D visualization of synaptic structures (neurons, vesicles, receptors, transporters, enzymes)
- Interactive controls to release neurotransmitters and toggle reuptake inhibition
- Emotional face display that reacts to neurotransmitter activity via mood parameters (happiness, arousal, anxiety)
- Info panel showing current effects and descriptions of active neurotransmitters

## Target Audience

High school and undergraduate biology/neuroscience students, educators, and anyone interested in understanding brain function through visual demonstration.
